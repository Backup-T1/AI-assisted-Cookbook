Welcome to the AI-assisted Recipie Browser


At this webpage, you can do the following:
  • Browse The Web for various Food Recipies
  • Save and View Saved Food Recipies
  • Use Motion Controls to navigate the webpage

To begin, click "Search Recipies" and search for a recipie, then hit the load button on whichever recipie you prefer.
Alternatively, you may choose to load a saved recipie from "Saved Recipies".
